# Code of Conduct

SysMender is for people who solve hard problems together. The community should feel like a good technical team: curious, practical, and respectful even when the diagnosis is difficult.

## Our expectations

- Be considerate and professional. Critique the implementation, not the person.
- Welcome questions from beginners and experienced administrators alike.
- Share reproducible evidence, explain assumptions, and admit uncertainty.
- Respect different platforms, accessibility needs, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Do not harass, threaten, dox, discriminate against, or personally attack others.
- Do not publish anyone's credentials, private logs, security findings, or identifying information.

## Scope

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and other official project spaces.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close threads, or limit access when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be consistent and proportionate to the behavior.

If you need to report conduct concerns privately, contact the repository owner through a private communication channel they have explicitly published. **A private moderation contact has not yet been configured.** Do not put sensitive personal details in a public issue.
